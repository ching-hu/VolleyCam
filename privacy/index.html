VolleyCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

VolleyCam is local-first. The developer does not sell user data, use ad tracking, or operate a server that receives core recordings and match data.

Local recording and permissions

Recording, match information, and settings are kept on the user’s devices unless the user chooses to save, share, or stream them. VolleyCam may request access to:

  • Camera to capture video.
  • Microphone to include audio in recordings and live streams.
  • Photos to save recordings and exported clips and to access media the user selects.
  • Local Network to connect Camera and Remote devices directly.
  • Motion to detect meaningful camera or tripod movement during recording.

Optional YouTube Live

YouTube Live is optional. Video, audio, visible scoreboard content, and broadcast information are sent to Google/YouTube only when the user starts streaming. Google/YouTube account authorization is used only to provide the YouTube streaming feature. VolleyCam does not receive the user’s Google password.

Optional tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. VolleyCam does not receive or store payment-card information.
